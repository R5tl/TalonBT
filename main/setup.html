WiFi setup

Join a network

Or use WPS (no phone needed)

Hold the BOOT button ~3 s on Talon (LED blinks white), then press your router's WPS button within two minutes — Talon joins and saves the network on its own. (Short-press BOOT any time to reopen this setup portal, LED breathes white.) You can also trigger WPS below.

After joining

Reconnect your phone to your own WiFi and find Talon at http://talon.local/ (or its IP from your router's device list — hostname "talon").
